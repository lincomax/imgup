First release of the imgup Android app.

- Share images to imgup from any app to upload them; several at once works too
- Share a link to an image (or a page with a preview image) and imgup downloads and uploads it
- Upload to ImgBB or Freeimage.host, chosen in Settings
- Copy, share, delete and open each upload from the app; delete happens in the app
- Links open in your default browser
- Dropdown arrows in Settings sit further in from the edge
